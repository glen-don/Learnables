Why stories work better than drills

Learnables is built on comprehensible input, the idea that you acquire language by understanding messages, not by memorizing rules. It's backed by over 40 years of research and it's how every polyglot you admire actually learned.

"We acquire language in one way, and only one way: when we understand messages."

- Stephen Krashen, linguist & researcher, University of Southern California

When you read a story with translation support, your brain does the work of connecting meaning to words. Naturally, without drilling. That's why words stick when you learn them in stories.

Common questions

Is this really free?
Yes. Every story in the library is free to start reading. 3 pages a day, any story, any level, forever. No trial that expires, no credit card, no ads. Premium just removes the daily page limit so you can read as much as you want.
What languages are available?
Currently Portuguese and Spanish, with more languages coming soon. We're one of the very few apps that teaches European Portuguese.
I'm a complete beginner. Will I understand anything?
Absolutely. Our easiest stories use simple sentences with full translations for every word and sentence. Tap anything you don't know. You'll be surprised how much you pick up just from reading.
How is this different from Duolingo?
Duolingo uses gamified drills and repetition. Learnables uses stories. You read real content with native audio and pick up vocabulary naturally through context. It's based on comprehensible input, the same method polyglots swear by.
